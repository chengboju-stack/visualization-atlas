把一条时间轴卷成螺旋 Rolling a timeline into a spiral

阿基米德螺旋 r = a + bθ 让「时间」同时沿角度和半径推进。拖动折叠滑块，看一条平铺的 365 天如何卷成一个周期结构。 An Archimedean spiral r = a + bθ advances time along angle and radius at once. Drag the folding slider to watch 365 flat days roll into a periodic structure.

折叠Folding

同一个角度位置上，相邻两圈对应相隔一年的同一天——这就是「周期」在极坐标里的样子。 Points at the same angle one turn apart are the same day one year later — that is what periodicity looks like in polar coordinates.
