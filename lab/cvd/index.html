Okabe–Ito 八色：横跨明度与色相两个维度，在三种色盲模拟下都还能区分。本网站的数据色板用的就是它。 The Okabe–Ito eight-colour set: varied in both lightness and hue, still separable under all three simulations. This site's data palette is built on it.

判断标准不是「看起来不一样」，而是任意两条颜色在模拟后仍然可分——所以还要配合直接标注、不同形状或线型，不能只靠颜色。 The test is not "they look different" but any two colours stay separable after simulation — which is why direct labels, shapes and line styles must back up colour.
