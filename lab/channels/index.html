同样是「长度」，为什么比「面积」好用？ Why is length easier to read than area?

Mackinlay 在 1986 年把视觉通道按感知精度排了个序。关键点不是这个名次本身，而是：换个任务，能用的通道就变了。下面切换三个任务，看哪些通道掉出可用范围。 In 1986 Mackinlay ranked visual channels by perceptual accuracy. The point is not the ranking itself but that changing the task changes which channels are usable. Switch tasks below and watch channels drop out.

通道可用性Channel availability

同一份数据，两种编码Same data, two encodings

两组数值完全相同。左边长度可以直接比长短；右边靠面积比大小，人眼对面积的判断误差明显更大——这就是「面积只适合粗略比较」的来源。 Both encode identical values. Length can be compared directly; area is judged far less accurately — which is why area is only good for coarse comparison.
