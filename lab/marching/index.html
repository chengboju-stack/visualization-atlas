等值线是怎么「走」出来的？ How does a contour get drawn?

Marching Squares 把平面切成小方格，每格只看四个角落在等值线的哪一侧，得到 16 种情形之一，再按查表结果连线。一格一格走过去，等值线就长出来了。 Marching Squares cuts the plane into cells, looks only at which side of the threshold the four corners fall on — 16 possible cases — and draws the corresponding segment. March cell by cell and the contour appears.
