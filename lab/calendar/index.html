日历热图：把一年折叠成一张纸 Calendar heatmap: a year on one sheet

van Wijk 的日历热图把 365 天排成 7×53 的格子，颜色编码数值。它比折线好在"哪一天"一找一个准，差在趋势不明显——所以常有下方那条年趋势线。框选一段试试联动。 The van Wijk calendar heatmap lays 365 days on a 7×53 grid, colour-encoding values. It beats a line chart at locating a specific day and loses at showing trend — hence the yearly trend line beneath. Brush a range to see them linked.
