屏幕到底能显示多少颜色？ How much colour can a screen show?

把 CIELAB 的 a*b* 平面按 L 切片扫一遍，凡是能换算回 sRGB 的点就上色——色域的形状自己就浮出来了。拖动亮度滑块，看可用区域怎么收窄。 Scan the CIELAB a*b* plane at a given L and colour every point that maps back into sRGB: the gamut silhouette appears on its own. Drag the lightness slider and watch the usable area narrow.
