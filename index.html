这门课不是把图表类型挨个背一遍，而是回答一个问题：面对一份数据，我该怎么把它变成看得懂的东西。下面这张图是五章之间的关系——从数据本身，到处理流程，到颜色这个基础通道，再到低维与时间两类图形。 This course does not ask you to memorise chart names. It answers one question: given a dataset, how do I turn it into something readable? The map below shows how the five chapters relate — from the data itself, through the pipeline, to colour as a base channel, and on to low-dimensional and temporal graphics.

CHAPTERS

五个章节Five chapters

每节都按「案例 → 理论 → 代码 → 演示」四层组织：先看真实问题，再看它背后的框架，然后读实现，最后自己动手。 Each section is organised in four layers: case → theory → code → demo. See the real problem, the framework behind it, the implementation, then try it yourself.

  1. 01

    数据Data

    从一份合成的新生数据出发：数据是什么、分几类、能做什么运算，以及 D3 怎么把数据变成 DOM。Starting from a synthetic freshman dataset: what data is, how it is typed, what operations it allows, and how D3 binds it to the DOM.

    5 节sections · DIKW · Stevens · Data-Join
  2. 02

    数据可视化流程Visualization Pipeline

    数据 → 变换 → 映射 → 渲染 → 交互。用一棵分类树走完整条链路，并实现 Reingold-Tilford 树布局与可折叠列表。Data → transform → map → render → interact. Walk the whole chain with a classification tree, then implement Reingold–Tilford layout and a collapsible list.

    4 节sections · Card · rollup · events
  3. 03

    色彩专题Color

    颜色空间、colormap 三族、Lab 色域、色盲模拟与科研经典配色。附一个能验证任意配色是否色盲友好的模拟器。Color spaces, three colormap families, Lab gamut, CVD simulation and research-grade palettes — plus a simulator that checks any palette.

    8 节sections · Lab · CVD · Okabe–Ito
  4. 04

    低维数据可视化Low-dimensional Data

    两个变量能玩出多少花样：坐标变换、十种曲线拟合、Cleveland 45° 倾角感知、Marching Squares 等值线。What two variables can show: coordinate transforms, ten curve fits, Cleveland's 45° angle, and Marching Squares contours.

    5 节sections · 45° · fit · contours
  5. 05

    时序数据可视化Time Series

    时间的五种属性与五种图形：折线、van Wijk 日历热图、阿基米德螺旋、Storyline 故事线。Five properties of time across five charts: line, van Wijk calendar heatmap, Archimedean spiral, and Storyline.

    5 节sections · calendar · spiral · Storyline

HOW TO USE

三种读法Three ways to read

沿着地图读Follow the map

从 01 开始按顺序走。每一节先读「案例」，再看「理论」，最后点开「演示」自己改参数。Start at 01 and go in order. Read the case, then the theory, then open the demo and change the parameters yourself.

带着问题查Look it up

先在上面的选型器里找到「我要表达什么关系」，再跳到对应章节，而不是从头翻。Find your relationship in the chooser above, then jump to the matching chapter instead of scrolling from the start.

直接动手Go hands-on

每节的「演示」都是真实运行的代码，不是录屏。改一个参数、看图形怎么变，比读十遍定义有用。Every demo is live code, not a recording. Change one parameter and watch the chart respond — worth ten readings of a definition.
